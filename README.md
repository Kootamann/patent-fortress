# patent-fortress

Quantum Patent Intelligence Platform.

A production-minded public patent intelligence company for quantum systems, AI security, and emerging technical domains.

This repository is the foundation for a real business: a public-source patent intelligence engine that tracks covered features, identifies white space, and produces counsel-ready opportunity briefs based on evidence, not hype.

Important operating principle:
- This project uses only public patent sources.
- Every record is tied to source provenance.
- Every opportunity is scored with confidence and review flags.
- No legal claim is treated as valid without human review.
- This is a research and strategy platform, not legal filing automation.

## Chosen business direction

We are building a company in the quantum patent intelligence category.

The product helps:
- quantum startups and labs
- researchers and technical teams
- patent counsel and IP strategists
- founders evaluating white-space opportunities

It answers:
- what is already covered in public patents?
- what feature combinations are under-served?
- where are the likely strategic opportunities?
- what technical areas deserve deeper review before filing?

## Product thesis

The company sells white-space intelligence for emerging technical domains.

The revenue model is:
- subscriptions for domain dashboards
- custom technical opportunity reports
- monitoring and alerting for new filings
- counsel-ready briefs and diligence support
- enterprise access for labs and IP teams

## Why quantum

Quantum is a strong first domain because it has:
- high technical novelty
- active public patent activity
- many adjacent but under-covered combinations
- strong interest from startups, researchers, and investors
- real strategic value in white-space discovery

## Source integrity policy

All outputs must be tied back to public evidence.

For every patent or claim family:
- official source URL
- jurisdiction
- publication date
- legal status
- extraction pipeline version
- confidence score
- review-required flag
