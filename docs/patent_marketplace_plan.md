# Patent Marketplace Business Plan

## Business concept

Build a marketplace where:
- patent holders can list patents and white-space opportunities
- buyers (startups, labs, enterprises) can discover, evaluate, and license patents
- transactions are facilitated with transparent valuation and legal support

This is a real, high-upside business model.

## Why this works

Market gap:
- patent holders have IP but no distribution channel
- buyers need IP but don't know where to find quality opportunities
- current patent markets are opaque, expensive, and fragmented
- there's no trusted "Airbnb for patents"

Opportunity:
- $3T in underutilized corporate patents
- thousands of startup IP portfolios with no monetization path
- growing demand for defensive and offensive patent strategies
- zero platforms that make patent discovery and licensing efficient

## Marketplace model

Core marketplace:
- patent listings with structured metadata
- buyer search and discovery
- valuation tools
- licensing agreement templates
- transaction facilitation
- escrow and payment handling

Revenue model:
- transaction fee: 10–20% of licensing deal value
- listing fees: $99–$999 per patent or portfolio
- premium seller features
- valuation and advisory services
- legal support and document templates

## Customer segments

Sellers:
- individual inventors
- startups with unused IP
- university spinouts
- corporate patent holders with non-core IP
- patent troll aggregators

Buyers:
- startups building defensively
- enterprises entering new markets
- smaller companies needing IP for licensing
- strategic acquirers
- investors evaluating IP-heavy companies

- 100 patents listed

Growth model (Year 3):

- $1.5M–$3M in transaction fees (15%)

- $200k in advisory and valuation services

Enterprise model (Year 5):

## Key success factors

1. Trust and credibility
- legal review of all transactions
- escrow and secure payment
- verified patent status
- dispute resolution process

2. AI-powered valuation
- use public patent data
- comparable sales analysis
- market demand signals
- litigation history and enforcement

3. Legal support
- standard licensing templates
- attorney network for complex deals
- opinion letter services
- IP counsel marketplace

4. Network effects
- more sellers attract buyers
- more buyers attract sellers
- data network value (marketplace analytics)

5. Integration with IP ecosystem
- connect to patent counsel
- integrate with IP management tools
- connect to venture and strategic buyers
- connect to patent financing platforms

## Competitive positioning

Current market:
- Patent brokers (expensive, high friction)
- Corporate patent licensing (opaque, slow)
- Patent auctions (limited supply, high reserve prices)
- Patent troll aggregators (negative reputation)

Your position:
- "Transparent, efficient patent licensing marketplace"
- "Airbnb for patents"
- Trusted, low-friction, fair-value discovery
- Built on real patent data and market signals

## Differentiation vs. existing solutions

vs. Patent brokers:
- lower cost (10% vs. 30%)
- faster (days vs. months)
- transparent (public marketplace vs. opaque negotiations)
- network effects

vs. Patent auctions:
- continuous supply (not periodic events)
- fair-value discovery (not reserve price gaming)
- smaller deal sizes (more accessible)
- flexible deal structures

vs. Corporate licensing:
- faster negotiation
- standardized terms
- transparent pricing
- lower friction

## Risk mitigation

Risks:
- patent validity disputes
- licensing disputes
- fraud or misrepresentation
- low transaction volume

Mitigation:
- IP counsel review of all listings
- standardized licensing agreements
- escrow and secure payment
- dispute resolution process
- strong incentives for repeat transactions

## Business model sensitivity

Unit economics (per transaction):
- average deal size: $100k
- marketplace fee: 10% = $10k per transaction
- cost to facilitate (legal, escrow, support): $2k
- gross margin per transaction: 80%

To $1M/month revenue:
- need 10 transactions/month at $100k average
- needs 100–200 active sellers
- needs strong buyer demand
- achievable in Year 2–3

To $10M/month revenue:
- need 100 transactions/month
- needs 1,000+ active sellers
- needs strong market reputation
- achievable in Year 5+
