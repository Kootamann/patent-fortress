# Business model

## Business concept

Patent White-Space Intelligence for technical sectors with emerging, under-covered public patent space.

## Primary business

The company sells insight and strategic guidance derived from public patent data.

It helps technical teams answer:
- what is already covered?
- what combinations are under-served?
- what domains merit deeper technical and legal review?
- which areas are likely to be strategically important next?

## Revenue model

### 1. Subscription model

For a monthly fee, customers get:
- domain dashboards
- saved search results
- coverage map over a technical area
- white-space opportunity ranking
- reports with evidence provenance

Suggested pricing:
- starter: $299–$999/month
- professional: $2k–$5k/month
- enterprise: $10k+/month

### 2. Custom report sales

Sell domain-specific, one-time intelligence packages.

Examples:
- quantum routing and entanglement gap report
- AI compliance patent map
- secure compute and cryptography opportunity brief

Pricing:
- $1k–$10k, depending on depth and source volume

### 3. Monitoring and alerts

Offer monitoring for new patent filings in a target domain.

This can be sold as:
- alert subscriptions
- weekly briefing reports
- monthly landscape summaries

### 4. Counsel support workflow

Provide structured internal research for patent attorneys before filing or licensing evaluation.

This is not legal advice, but a useful strategy support layer.

## Ideal first customer profiles

- quantum startups with hardware or middleware focus
- technical founders exploring patent strategy
- IP teams for emerging technology companies
- university spinouts and research groups
- investors evaluating innovation space

## Why this is a real business

The value is in operational intelligence, not in making magical legal claims.

Customers pay for:
- evidence-backed landscape analysis
- source-aware reports
- technical feature mapping
- faster market understanding
- more disciplined review of patent opportunity space

## Early customer acquisition strategy

- create 3–5 sample domain reports
- target early-stage quantum and AI startups
- offer a free or low-cost pilot report
- build credibility through source-backed, explainable outputs
- turn pilot customers into recurring subscription clients

## Risk management

- no automatic patentability claims
- no filing without counsel review
- all reports include source evidence
- use explicit confidence labels
- make the workflow transparent and reviewable

## First 90-day objective

By day 90, the company should have:
- 1 chosen domain
- 5 pilot reports
- 1 monetized report or pilot subscription
- an initial customer list and sales message
- a credible SaaS foundation for scaling
